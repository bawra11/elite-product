Experience DNA
we LOVE all shapes
not a rating, It's CHARACTER of the place

This is elite's portrait of a restaurant, a radar built from what diners actually mention.

It does not answer “is this place good?” It answers “what is this place like?”

So a guest can walk in with eyes open, and the recommendation can still stand.

Star Ratings collapse a visit into one number. A 4.2 can mean excellent food with terrible parking, or average food that is easy to reach. Guests still get surprised. Hosts still get punished for a trait that is not a failure of hospitality.

Experience DNA separates character from recommendation. A kitchen can be exceptional and still sit on a street with no parking. That is not a 3-star restaurant. That is a DISTINCTIVE SHAPE.
