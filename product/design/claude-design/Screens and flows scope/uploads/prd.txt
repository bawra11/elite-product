1. Purpose, Audience and Decision Language
This PRD defines what Elite should build in Phase 1 and Phase 2, how each module should behave, which user
roles can perform which actions, how money and entitlements move, what events must be instrumented, and
what conditions must be satisfied before launch. It is intended to be implementation-ready enough for
engineering estimation and solution design while preserving explicitly open commercial, legal and algorithmic
decisions. Version 1.7 makes two product rules explicit: a restaurant may charge the diner a restaurant-defined
membership fee for its Restaurant Membership, and Elite diner levels are assigned from the diner’s cumulative
confirmed Earned Tokens only, regardless of whether those Earned Tokens are later spent or expire.

Confirmed decisions incorporated in this PRD
• Identity includes mobile number, name, gender and date of birth, with appropriate consent and privacy controls.
• Restaurant loyalty uses 1 point = ₹1. Each restaurant can configure its own expiry policy and terms; if no outlet-specific expiry is
configured, points expire 365 days after issuance.
• Elite Membership costs ₹199/month and includes 3 member Amplifies each month. Each Amplify is priced/stated at ₹49, for ₹147 of
monthly Amplify value.
• The Amplify prompt appears immediately after a diner publishes an eligible experience.
• Restaurants may amplify eligible positive experiences through paid batches starting at ₹50,000 + GST.
• Restaurant partner accounts require a Marketing Workspace; agencies require a multi-outlet portal, Master Central Balance, outlet
allocations, milestone rewards and ROI reporting.
• Phase 2 supports both public meetups accessible to all eligible users and private meetups for a host’s own community.
• Meetup hosts must be active Elite Members with a fully completed and verified profile. Participant-facing pricing must disclose total
joining price and the amount redeemable at the restaurant; host earnings are not separately itemised to participants.
• Version 1.3 added legacy-data migration and rollback, child/age controls, consent and data-rights operations, report-abuse protection,
intermediary/UGC moderation, non-Bridge visit verification, bill mismatch handling and meetup safety gates.
• Version 1.4 added diner-created public/private Curations V1, verified Google restaurant import, a 15-restaurant cap and Verified Visited
badges.
• Version 1.5 adds verified Token earning bound to unique visits, paid Token packs, separate Token lots/expiry, contextual top-ups, closed-
loop restrictions, anti-pay-to-win safeguards and engagement experiments.
• A restaurant may make its Restaurant Membership free or charge the diner a restaurant-defined membership fee. The fee, billing term,
renewal, benefits, taxes and cancellation/refund terms must be shown before payment; no paid membership is activated without explicit
diner confirmation and successful payment.
• Elite levels use Lifetime Earned Tokens: the cumulative confirmed Tokens earned from qualifying activity. Spending or expiry does not
reduce level progress. Purchased, Pack Bonus and Promotional Tokens never count toward levels; reversals remove the associated earned
progress.

PART I — PRODUCT STRATEGY & PHASE PLAN
2. Product Vision
Vision
Make every dining decision more informed, every restaurant visit more effortless, and every honest diner
contribution more meaningful.
Elite is the live, experience-led layer for dining. Diners discover restaurants through recent experiences and
real-time context, use smart utilities during the visit, contribute back to a trusted community, receive
personalised value, and build an ongoing relationship with restaurants and other diners.
Problems to solve
 Static star ratings preserve historical reputation but do not reliably represent the current experience.
 Thoughtful diner contributions are quickly buried and do not create lasting status, reach or influence for the
contributor.
 Listing marketplaces can reward paid visibility over recency, relevance and trust.
 Restaurant discounts are often blanket subsidies rather than tools for first visit, repeat visit, recovery, higher
spend or low-demand periods.
 Restaurants and their marketing agencies lack a closed-loop system connecting campaigns to verified visits,
payments, reservations, loyalty and repeat behaviour.
 People who want to dine around a community or interest still coordinate fragmented venue discovery,
participant collection and payment outside the dining platform.
The Elite closed loop
1. Discover through recent experiences, curations, people, events and context.
2. Validate through Live Menu, Live Vibe, deals, availability, reservations and current sentiment.
3. Visit, reserve, order, pay or self-checkout through Elite-supported flows.
4. Share a verified or complete experience and receive visible contribution impact.
5. Build status through helpfulness, following, Tokens, membership and Amplify.
6. Return through restaurant loyalty, personalised offers, recovery and lifecycle communication.
7. In Phase 2, create or join dining meetups and let restaurants compete to host relevant groups.

Recommendation must balance relevance, contribution quality, freshness and diversity. Paid curator placement

profiles.
8.3 Restaurant Event Ads in the Story Rail
Restaurant event campaigns may use the story rail as a paid placement. The event tile can use event artwork
cropped for the circular treatment, but it must remain visibly different from a person profile and must carry
Event or Promoted Event disclosure. Selecting it opens the event detail page defined in Section 22.

47. Migration and Backward Compatibility
Highest-priority upgrade rule / The Phase 1 release is an upgrade to an operating platform, not a greenfield
launch. Legacy experiences, users, balances, loyalty lots, reservations, payments and restaurant Scores must
have explicit provenance, reconciliation, dual-run/cutover and rollback behaviour. Financial or identity data
is never “rolled back” by destructive deletion; forward-compatible corrections are used.
47.1 Migration principles
 Preserve stable source and target IDs or maintain a permanent mapping table; every migrated record has
source_system, source_schema_version, migration_batch_id and migrated_at.
 Do not invent verification. A legacy experience is retro-verified only when deterministic historical evidence
links it to an eligible payment, reservation/check-in, table verification or approved bill record.
 No diner loses restaurant loyalty, Elite Tokens, paid entitlement, reservation value or payment/refund state
because of migration.
 No public restaurant Score/label switches to the new model until shadow calculations, data-quality checks,
restaurant-level diff review and rollback rehearsal pass.
 Use dual-run/canary rather than a single big-bang cutover for Score, experiences, identity and financial
projections.
 All migration scripts are idempotent, resumable, dry-run capable and produce per-entity and aggregate
reconciliation reports.

Elite
Elite is not simply a restaurant listing, review or discount app. Phase 1 creates a closed-loop dining network
connecting discovery, live context, verified experience, personalised value, loyalty, payment, creator reach and
restaurant/agency marketing. Phase 2 adds trusted public and private dining communities where members can
organise gatherings, restaurants can compete to host them, and Elite can manage joining, payment, attendance and
settlement end-to-end.

3. Old and new Score run in shadow; a restaurant changing label has an audited recalculation record with

duplicate financial entries.
5. In-flight reservation remains visible once and is owned by exactly one writer through completion.

dependent processing stops.
