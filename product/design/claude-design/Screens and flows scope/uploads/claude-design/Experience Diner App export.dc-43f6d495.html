Verified visit · 14 Aug, 11:40pm
Follow

Came for one drink, stayed for the kitchen. The slow lamb kofta is the reason to book — charred outside, still loose in the middle, and the tahini underneath does actual work. The muhammara is worth ordering twice.

Ask for the corner banquette. The bar side is loud after 9:30 and the room does nothing to absorb it. Parking is street-only — I walked from the metro.
